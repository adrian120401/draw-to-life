# Playroom

Targets: draw_to_life/ui.py, draw_to_life/preview.py,
draw_to_life/web/scene.css, draw_to_life/web/character-player.js.

Mode: Experience. Children and adults giving a drawing life locally.
Approved: upload automatically → real analysis/generation stages → play.
Food, affection, and play are visual interactions without persistent needs.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The drawing becomes a companion. One task per screen, without a
masthead, settings panel, explanatory copy, or a side-by-side workbench.

OWN-WORLD: Pale lilac fills the viewport; lavender organic shapes, small pink
circles and butter-yellow stars frame the edges. Cream surfaces and large
violet controls carry only the actions. Local Trebuchet typography.

STORY: Upload a drawing, see it being understood and created, then touch and
feed the resulting character. Actual pipeline phases drive the waiting copy.

FIRST VIEWPORT: One centered photo token above “Subí tu dibujo”, with generous
empty space. In the final screen a large character leads above three colorful
buttons: Comida, Mimos, Jugar. Restart and download remain secondary.

FORM: User-pinned sequential playroom; code-led implementation of the approved
plan. No concept lottery: the user specified the sequence and palette and
authorized improvising its geometric background.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance. Existing mascot artwork retains its generation provenance;
expression textures are procedural assets made from the existing face layers.
